About

I am an independent researcher based in Jakarta, Indonesia. Before moving into energy economics, I spent 12 years in Indonesia's upstream oil and gas sector at CNOOC SES and Pertamina Hulu Energi OSES, working in production engineering, artificial lift, project evaluation, and field operations.

I completed an MBA at Carnegie Mellon University's Tepper School of Business, with the Energy & Sustainability track and coursework in finance, economics, operations, and quantitative methods. My current research focuses on hydrogen economics and power-sector costs. I plan to pursue doctoral study in energy economics and policy.

Current Work

Working paper on SSRN · submitted for peer review

What Does a Green Hydrogen Mandate Cost? Evidence from the Intermountain Power Project

A techno-economic study of hydrogen production at the Intermountain Power Project. The paper compares grid-connected and renewable-only production pathways and estimates the cost imposed by the project's green hydrogen requirement. Submitted to the International Journal of Hydrogen Energy.
