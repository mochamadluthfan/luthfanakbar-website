I study the costs and trade-offs of decarbonizing energy systems. My current work focuses on hydrogen and power-sector economics. I also plan to study the economics of electric vehicles and plug-in hybrids.

Publications & Working Papers

Submitted for peer review

What Does a Green Hydrogen Mandate Cost? Evidence from the Intermountain Power Project

Submitted to the International Journal of Hydrogen Energy

This paper studies the cost of producing hydrogen at the Intermountain Power Project under a renewable-only requirement. Using roughly 17,500 hours of wholesale electricity price, weather, and grid-carbon data, I compare a grid-connected pathway with a renewable-only pathway supported by hydrogen storage. The renewable-only pathway carries a $4.37/kg (113%) cost premium in the model, driven mainly by lower electrolyzer utilization rather than storage cost. The analysis also estimates an implied carbon abatement cost of roughly $510 per tonne of CO2.
