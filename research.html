Research

Energy transition economics

I study the costs and trade-offs of decarbonizing energy systems. My current work focuses on hydrogen and power-sector economics. I also plan to study the economics of electric vehicles and plug-in hybrids.

Current and Planned Research

Hydrogen & power-sector economics

Techno-economic modeling of hydrogen production, electrolyzer utilization, electricity costs, storage, and renewable integration. Further work based on the Intermountain Power Project analysis is planned.

Indonesia's energy transition

Exploratory work on floating solar, data-center electricity demand, and pumped-storage hydropower in the Cirata and Jatiluhur system in West Java.

Electric vehicle economics

Planned research comparing the economics of plug-in hybrids, battery electric vehicles, and internal-combustion vehicles. A first supporting analysis is available as Research Note 01.
