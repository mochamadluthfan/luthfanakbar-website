Research Experience

What Does a Green Hydrogen Mandate Cost? Evidence from the Intermountain Power Project 2026

Independent Researcher

  • Built a techno-economic optimization model comparing grid-sourced, dedicated-renewable, and partial-mandate hydrogen production at the Intermountain Power Project, using two years of hourly CAISO SP-15 price data, site-calibrated solar and wind generation, and hourly grid carbon intensity, validated against the project's own disclosed financials.
  • Quantified the cost premium of a renewable-only supply mandate relative to grid-sourced supply, decomposed that premium into its underlying drivers (energy cost, asset utilization, and storage) to identify which lever matters most for cost reduction, and converted the finding into an implied carbon-abatement cost to benchmark the mandate against conventional carbon-pricing policy.
  • Working paper posted on SSRN (DOI: 10.2139/ssrn.7428658); submitted to the International Journal of Hydrogen Energy, currently under peer review. Described further on the Research page.
